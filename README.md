# Matthew Aldous · B.Eng (Hons)

**Technical Lead · Cloud Engineering · AI & Automation · DevOps**  
Melbourne, Australia

Senior engineer and technical lead delivering enterprise software, cloud platforms and automation. My work spans education, FinTech, customer data, telecommunications, gaming networks and media delivery — connecting architecture with reliable implementation and operations.

[Portfolio](https://aldous.info/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matthewaldous/) · [CV](https://aldous.info/Matthew-Aldous-Resume-2026.pdf) · [Email](mailto:matthew.aldous@gmail.com)

## Experience in practice

- Designed and deployed an **ISO 27001-aligned solution for 250+ schools and 100,000+ end users**.
- Delivered platforms supporting **more than $3 billion in capital** between brokers and lenders.
- Led enterprise delivery and engineering teams, including **7-person and 12-person teams**.
- Engineered APAC gaming networks and Australian CDN services serving **millions of users and viewers**.
- Authored the primary data analysis for Australia's first 5G network location and coverage maps.
- Co-founded **Suburbia.net**, one of Australia's longest-running free public access systems.
